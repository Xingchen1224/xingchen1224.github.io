## Professional Profile

I am a technical leader specialising in **image algorithms research and development** and
**computer vision engineering**. My work sits at the intersection of rigorous algorithmic
thinking and practical product delivery — designing imaging pipelines, directing R&D
initiatives, and helping teams ship high-quality visual systems at scale.

I am driven by hard problems in computational imaging and enjoy the challenge of turning
cutting-edge research into reliable, production-ready software.
